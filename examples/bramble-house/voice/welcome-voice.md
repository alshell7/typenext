# The voice of Bramble House

Fictional writing brief and examples for the Bramble House welcome note.

The welcome note addresses guests directly, using everyday language and a warm, unhurried voice. It gives clear practical details without sales language or promises about creativity.

Example: "There is room here for an unfinished thought. Bring a notebook, find a chair, and begin wherever you are."

Example: "Join the walk if you feel like company. The reading room is there if you would rather stay with your page."

The intended audience is adults who enjoy writing. Some are finishing a draft; others are starting again after a long pause.
