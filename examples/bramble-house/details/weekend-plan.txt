Bramble House weekend plan
Fictional sample material for TypeNext.

Friday supper begins at 19:00 in the kitchen.
Saturday breakfast begins at 08:30 in the kitchen.
The Saturday writing session begins at 09:30 in the shared writing room.
The Saturday coastal walk begins at 14:00 by the blue orchard gate.
The walk lasts forty minutes and follows the sheltered bay.
Sunday breakfast begins at 08:30 in the kitchen.
The Sunday reading circle begins at 11:00 in the shared writing room.
Guests leave on Sunday at 15:00.

Every session is optional. There is no evening workshop on Saturday.
