# Bramble House

Fictional sample material for trying TypeNext. These details describe an invented writing retreat.

Guests arrive at Bramble House on Friday at 16:00.

Bramble House has six bedrooms and a shared writing room overlooking the orchard. The writing room holds twelve people. There is no projector or stage.

The reading room stays quiet until 11:00 each morning. Tea, coffee and drinking water are available in the kitchen throughout the day.

The orchard gate is painted blue. The house sits above a sheltered bay, ten minutes on foot from the village.

Guests leave on Sunday at 15:00. The weekend has no required word count; a few honest sentences are enough.
