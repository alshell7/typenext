# The coastal walk

Fictional sample material for TypeNext.

The coastal walk starts at the blue orchard gate and ends at the same gate. It follows a gravel path above the sheltered bay, passing a small stone bench.

The route is two kilometres long and has three steps near the bench. The sea can be heard from the orchard, even on a still afternoon.

If it rains, guests can spend the afternoon in the reading room instead. Bramble House has a shelf of poetry, essays and local field guides.
