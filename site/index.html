Begin with your intention.

Name a note and add a little background. Your first line can set the objective.

Bring the details that matter.

Create named contexts from folders, files, websites and live notes. Attach several to a note, and reuse them in another. Shared reference text is stored once.

Keep only what fits.

Tab accepts a continuation. Ctrl or Command plus Right accepts a word. Escape lets the thought go.

Your writing stays with you.

Start with short offline writing prompts, or recall phrases from your writing and references. Attachments are optional. Ctrl or Command plus Space opens a few ways forward; a local model can provide richer continuations.

OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, and custom endpoints are optional. Choose an external model explicitly to keep its suggestions active. With a hosted chat model, Ctrl or Command plus Space requests up to three fresh choices in one call. Previewing a choice makes no further requests. You can return to local at any time.

Speak while you write with optional Whistle dictation, a one-time 17 MB download. Choose your microphone; Ctrl or Command plus Shift plus D starts and stops. A clip lasts up to 30 seconds and is transcribed on your device. Words enter the note when you stop, and automatic suggestions resume when enabled.

Make it your own.

TypeNext is licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later. The app uses Tauri, React, and a small Markdown editor. Download a small optional writing model inside TypeNext, or connect your own local server. Your words lead; suggestions wait for your acceptance.

To build the desktop app, install Node.js 22.13 or later, stable Rust, and the Tauri prerequisites for your system.

git clone https://github.com/alshell7/typenext.git
cd typenext
npm ci
npm run tauri:dev
